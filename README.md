# Meanwhile, in L.A.

A street atlas of fictional Los Angeles. React, TypeScript, Vite, and MapLibre GL JS; hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Develop

```sh
npm ci
npm run dev
```

OpenFreeMap vector tiles supply the paper basemap, with OpenMapTiles/OpenStreetMap attribution. No API keys or backend are required.

## Interactions

- Search people, places, titles, and addresses; browse Places, People, or Routes. Title searches ignore periods, including `LA` / `L.A.`.
- Filter by film, location type, deaths, or current map view. Movie tabs in place details and map labels immediately filter to that title and fit its mapped locations into view. The movie selector and a single Show all action stay visible while viewing filtered details. There are no separate × buttons for clearing movie or text filters. Show all also returns the map to the city view.
- Switch between Map and Satellite. Satellite uses Esri World Imagery with the atlas labels, location icons, routes, and county fog above it. Shared links retain the chosen view; Show all clears filters while preserving it.
- Open each real filming address in Google Maps. Filming addresses are labeled separately when the story position differs.
- Open paired movie stills and real-site photographs, enlarge either, and expand Sources for credits and citations. Capture dates appear when verified; undated and archival photographs are retained without claiming they are current. Aerial photographs are explicitly labeled.
- Use the small arrows beneath a movie image to browse additional location views. Shared places retain separate frames and galleries for each film or series; screen thumbnails preserve the full frame, and image enlargement credits the frame currently shown.
- Verified home properties have direct Zillow links beside their filming-site Maps link. Building pages represent the whole building, with no invented apartment unit. Links refer to the real filming property, including when the fictional story uses another address.
- The compass follows map rotation; tap it to return north. Zooming out stops at a county overview fitted to the map’s current width, height, and rotation. Islands stay outside that overview until a researched location reveals them.
- Use My location to request browser geolocation and plot a position and accuracy circle. Coordinates are not saved in shared links or application storage. A secure origin and browser/OS permission are required.
- Spoilers is off by default. Map icons retain their category shapes and movie colors with spoilers hidden. Black marker strokes cover only revealing words and phrases in lists, detail pages, marker labels, hover tips, route stops, people associations and source metadata. Ordinary names, addresses, dates and surrounding prose stay readable; a name is covered only when it reveals a plot point. Revealing screen images remain covered. Each text and photo cover gets a separate generated ink outline and angle, stable across rerenders. Check Spoilers to restore the full content. The preference is saved locally, stays unchanged by Show all, and is never enabled by a shared link. Redacted stills cannot be enlarged. Browser checks include the ink edges in the directory, map hover tips and image covers at desktop and phone widths.
- Filter canonical story dates with a range, decade preset, or playback. Returning both endpoints to their limits restores All time.
- Share selections and filters with hash links. Phones can switch between the map and directory.
- Open a person’s page to see their locations. Locations preserve their originating person or route so Back returns to the same page.
- Routes displays every matching route in a different movie color; route stops have location pages and photographs. Route connectors link documented scenes, not verified continuous travel.
- Movie colors apply to location icons; routes have distinct colors. Labels appear on hover, keyboard focus, or selection; there are no always-on featured labels. Hovered and selected labels render above other location icons. Deaths appear as full-size, separately selectable entries in All and Deaths. Death pages list the victims. People pages retain participants’ links to standalone death scenes; derived death entries do not misassociate surviving event participants.
- The territory outside the county is solid black with a feathered boundary. One canvas mask covers the basemap and its labels, so roads, place names, and imagery fade out together. It follows pan, zoom, and resize; both map modes share it. Islands remain masked until a researched place establishes one as part of the atlas.

## Data

`research/locations-100.json` preserves the initial 100-entry catalog: 96 places and four routes. Three unmatched production geocodes remain outside the prototype directory. The original directory contains 93 places; unresolved story addresses remain searchable without a false precise pin. `research/expansion-100.json` aggregates 100 further researched places from the three `expansion-*.json` source manifests. `route-revisions.json` and `route-extra.json` add eight further places for LAX and route stops. Heat’s robbery and meeting locations are supplemented in `heat-robberies.json` and `heat-lives.json`; `heat-routes.json` keeps the Cheritto branch of the bank escape. The ambulance theft, armored-truck robbery and Johnie’s Broiler are separate places without a connecting route. The T2 channel chase is one event at the documented bridge/gate site, not a route; its original gate photo pair is retained in the event’s Sources and component research. Old route links redirect to the corresponding event. `franks.json` records the diner’s eligible shared appearances and its rejected non-L.A. roles. `player-strange-days.json` and `speed-crash.json` add the requested films with story-first placements, paired photographs, and a segmented Speed bus route. `smoke-house.json` distinguishes Argo’s named Smoke House from La La Land’s Lipton’s exterior/interior composite; `voyager-locations.json` records the crew’s other 1996 city stops. `argo-locations.json` expands the Hollywood cover-film operation while excluding Los Angeles production sites that play Tehran or Washington; the film’s Burbank studio is distinguished from the historical Studio Six address. `live-die-chase.json` keeps supported downtown/river chase sections separate from an unlocated freeway sequence. `revisedPlaces` updates existing shared locations without duplicating the physical site.

Run `node scripts/prepare-data.mjs` after editing research. `scripts/enrich-data.mjs` applies story geography, dates, deaths, expansion manifests, route revisions, and photograph manifests to `src/data/atlas.json`. `research/geocodes.json` preserves production coordinates; `research/story-geography.json` records conflicting story evidence and its sources. Story positions take precedence. `research/story-map-corrections.json` replaces seven whole-street building highlights with location icons. Fictional address numbers can supply an approximate street interpolation; street-only references use a representative street point, explicitly marked approximate. Aunt Ruth’s point follows the screenplay’s Fountain-to-Sunset segment. These are display estimates, not identified parcels. Deckard’s apartment uses an explicitly labeled editorial downtown approximation; its canonical street remains unknown and Ennis House stays the separately labeled filming address. Broad inferences use dashed areas; unresolved city-only places have no pin. Uncontradicted filming locations remain proxies, rather than independently established canonical addresses. Dashed routes connect selected stops, not turn-by-turn paths.

`research/expansion2-100.json` aggregates another 100 distinct places (IDs 400–499) from the `expansion2-crime`, `expansion2-classic`, `expansion2-lives`, and `expansion2-tv` manifests, which brought the directory to 359 places in that batch. Its 12 TV additions are recurring landmarks: the original Baywatch headquarters and Mitch’s canal home, Rockford’s trailer, New Girl’s loft and bar, the three Modern Family homes, the Banks mansion, Fisher & Sons, Casa Walsh, and the Peach Pit. No episode-only TV stops are added. The Banks and Walsh homes follow their story addresses; the Peach Pit has a labeled western-L.A. estimate rather than a Pasadena pin. New Girl’s bar keeps its exterior and interior components together. Research audits preserve exclusions, uncertain placements, geocoder matches, and photo limitations. All additions have source-linked screen/site pairs; archive, undated, and aerial photographs are labeled accordingly.

`research/north-county.json` adds the original Agua Dulce ranch/park sites from Nope and the recurring Hammond home from Santa Clarita Diet. Universal’s later Jupiter’s Claim rebuild is separate from the original story site. Northern filming stand-ins with unsupported local story geography remain in the research audit. Craig and Gina Pomeroy’s recurring Baywatch loft is documented with official episode frames in `research/baywatch-craig.json`; its production address remains unverified, so it is a research candidate without a map pin.

The (500) Days of Summer bench is named Angels Knoll and can be found by searching Angels Flight, the neighboring railway. This does not assert a railway ride in that film.

Offscreen deaths are included when a cited source establishes them. `research/terminator-police.json` records the Central Division station attack and keeps the demolished Vernon production site in provenance only. Central Division / West Highland does not establish a story street address, so the station remains searchable with photographs and deaths but has no precise map pin.

Dates establish presence during a story, not continuous tenancy. Explicit dates override release years. For otherwise contemporary stories, release year is an editorial estimate displayed with `c.`. Falling Down uses June 12, 1991 from the official Cannes synopsis; Blade Runner remains in November 2019. Future dates are not replaced by production years.

`research/media-a.json`, `media-b.json`, and `media-recent.json` record photograph sources, credits, capture dates when known, and component distinctions. Actual movie frames and local site JPEGs are under `public/photos`; `credits.json` preserves provenance. Some buildings have been demolished, and current-site photographs may show their replacements or demolition. A site's image can depict a production component rather than its story geography. When per-title photographs are available, movie and time filters select the corresponding frame. Shared sites retain an explicitly named screen image so it is not silently attributed to another film.

`public/la-county.geojson` is a simplified US Census TIGERweb county boundary, including islands; provenance is in `research/county-boundary.json`. `public/story-streets.geojson` preserves OpenStreetMap reference geometry, with its query recorded in `research/street-geometry.json`; the map no longer draws entire named roads for individual buildings.

`research/spoiler-audit.json` records exact spoiler phrases by place/route ID and field path, with separate fields for revealing screen images. Per-title selectors preserve innocent appearances at shared sites. There are no whole-record text redactions. Generic outcome words are replaced individually, and death-victim associations remain concealed. `src/spoilers.mjs` creates presentation copies; canonical facts, IDs and map geometry remain unchanged. Redacted text is replaced before rendering, including accessible labels, rather than hidden underneath CSS. Add new non-death reveals to the audit when extending the catalog.

`research/property-links-early.json` and `research/property-links-late.json` record direct Zillow matches and audited omissions for every home candidate. Demolished properties, approximate addresses and unverified pages do not get substitute links. `research/media-improvements.json` records additional film/TV frames and their original image/source URLs. Gallery frames retain individual spoiler flags.

`research/eady-bosch-parcel.json` preserves the official county geometry for parcel 5556-015-004 at 1870 Blue Heights Drive. The county-mapped house footprint locates the pin within that parcel and replaces the interpolated point about 120 metres away; both story and production pins are corrected. The house's Maps action uses the full verified street address instead of coordinates.
